## Motivation (draft, ~140 words)

Live cricket draws some of the largest sporting audiences in India,
but the information shown to spectators inside a stadium is still
dominated by low-density LED scoreboards: score, overs, batter runs,
little else. Broadcast viewers, in contrast, see win-probability
graphs, player comparisons, phase breakdowns, and commentary that
turns raw data into story. The gap matters: engagement research shows
that a spectator who understands *why* a passage of play is
significant stays attentive for longer and talks about it more. An
AI-augmented spectator board narrows this gap. By combining a
real-time stats engine, a trained win-probability model, and a
template-guarded LLM commentator, a venue can deliver broadcast-grade
context on the big screen without a human analyst. This project
builds and evaluates such a board for T20 cricket, using IPL replays
to make every participant see the identical match.
