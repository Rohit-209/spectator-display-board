# Section 1 — Problem Definition

## Motivation (~145 words)

Live cricket draws some of the largest sporting audiences in India,
yet the information presented to spectators inside a stadium is still
dominated by low-density LED scoreboards: score, overs, batter runs,
little else. Broadcast viewers see what in-ground viewers do not —
win-probability graphs, player and team comparisons, phase
breakdowns, and commentary that turns raw data into story. The gap
matters: engagement research shows that a spectator who understands
*why* a passage of play is significant stays attentive for longer and
talks about it more [1]. An AI-augmented spectator board narrows this
gap. By combining a real-time stats engine, a trained
win-probability model, and a template-guarded LLM commentator [2],
venues can deliver broadcast-grade context on the big screen without
a human analyst. This project builds and evaluates such a board for
T20 cricket, using IPL replays so every participant sees the
identical match.
