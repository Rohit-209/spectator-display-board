## Reference ideas to populate [1], [2], [4]

Pick two for §1 (problem motivation) and one for §3 (methodology
anchor). Suggested themes with real anchor papers worth searching:

- **Win-probability modelling in cricket:** Davis, Perera & Swartz on
  Duckworth-Lewis-Stern / WASP; Bailey & Clarke on in-play chase
  prediction; Hubáček et al. on ML in cricket forecasting.
- **Hallucination mitigation in LLMs:** retrieval-augmented
  generation (Lewis et al., NeurIPS 2020); constrained-decoding or
  tool-use papers from 2023–2025 that are specifically about
  factuality guard-rails.
- **Glanceable / stadium-display design:** Vogel & Balakrishnan on
  interactive public ambient displays; any ACM CHI PLAY paper on
  stadium audience experience; Nielsen Norman Group write-ups on
  10-foot UI as supporting references (don't lean on them alone —
  pair with a peer-reviewed source).
- **IPL viewership / spectator engagement news:** BARC-India or
  JioHotstar press releases; Hindustan Times / ESPNCricinfo for a
  citable news reference (cite as web, with URL and access date).
- **System-level reference:** cite Next.js, scikit-learn, or SQLite
  only if you reference a specific feature; otherwise skip (the
  rubric is for *data and claims*, not framework name-drops).
