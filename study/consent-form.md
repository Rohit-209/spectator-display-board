## What this study is about

You will watch a short replay of a cricket match on a projected display
and answer six questions about what you see. After the replay you will
fill in two short questionnaires and a six-question quiz about the match.
Total time ≈ 45 minutes.

The purpose is to measure whether the display is easy to read, easy to
understand, and whether its AI-generated commentary is useful.

## What we will record

- Your answers to the on-screen tasks (verbal; written down by the observer).
- Your responses on the paper questionnaires.
- Your approximate age bracket and self-rated cricket knowledge.
- Session timing (how long each task takes).

We will **not** record your name against any of this data. Each
participant is referred to only by a code (e.g. `P07`).

## Your rights

- Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any point without giving
  a reason, and your partial data will be destroyed if you ask.
- There is no reward or penalty for taking part.
- Anonymised data (participant code only) may appear in the final report
  and its appendices.
- You can request a copy of the final report by emailing the investigator.

## Risks

The study involves watching a projected screen at close range (3 m) for
about 30 seconds during the legibility test, and standing at 10 m
briefly. If you have any visual condition that makes this uncomfortable,
please tell the investigator — we can skip that step.
