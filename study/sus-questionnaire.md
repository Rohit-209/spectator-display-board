## Scoring (facilitator only — do not show to participant)

- Odd items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): subtract 1 from the score.
- Even items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): subtract score from 5.
- Sum the ten adjusted values and multiply by 2.5 → **SUS score /100**.

Benchmarks: 68 ≈ average; 72–85 "good"; 85+ "excellent".
