# Legibility protocol — 3 m / 6 m / 10 m

Run this **before** the task block, while the participant is unfamiliar
with the board layout — this prevents them answering from memory rather
than from what they can actually read.

Participant code: `P___`     Date: ___________

## Setup

- Board projected at 1920×1080 on a plain wall.
- Mark three positions on the floor with masking tape: **3 m**, **6 m**,
  **10 m** from the projected image, all perpendicular to the screen.
- Lighting: normal room lights **on** (the deployment target is a common
  room, not a dark auditorium). Note the lux reading if a meter is
  handy, otherwise record qualitatively.
- Load a 2nd-innings replay and **pause** at an over where Zones A and E
  are both populated (score strip visible, an insight on screen).
- Participants wear whatever vision correction they would normally use.

Light level: [ ] bright [ ] normal [ ] dim    Lux (if measured): ______

## Script

**Facilitator reads aloud:** "I'll stand you at three distances from
the screen. At each distance I'll point to one part of the board and
ask you to read it out. If you can't read it, just say so — there's no
penalty."

For each (distance × zone) cell, record one of:

- **R** — read correctly (every digit / word right)
- **P** — partial (gist right, one wrong digit / one wrong word)
- **N** — couldn't read

Also stopwatch the **time to read** (speak the full phrase) at each
cell; cap at 15 s.

## Grid — Zone A (score strip)

Read aloud the whole top line: team short code, score, overs, target
equation.

## Follow-up — colour discrimination (10 m only)

Still at 10 m, point to the Zone D win-probability bars and ask:
"Which team's bar is which?" Record whether the participant can
distinguish the two bars by colour **and** label (the labels are what
we rely on for colour-blind safety).
