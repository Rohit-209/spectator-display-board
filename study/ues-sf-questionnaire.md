## Adaptation note (for the report)

This is the O'Brien, Cairns & Hall (2018) 12-item UES-SF with "the
application" / "the website" replaced by "the display" and
"the match on this display"; the Reward item "I continued to use
the application out of curiosity" is dropped and replaced by a
word-of-mouth item (RW-3) because the display is a passive viewing
experience, not an interactive application.

## Scoring (facilitator only — do not show to participant)

- **PU items are reverse-coded**: compute `(6 - raw)` before averaging
  so higher = better experience across all subscales.
- Average each subscale (FA, PU, AE, RW) to 1–5.
- Overall engagement = mean of the four subscale means.
